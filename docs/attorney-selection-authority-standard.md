# Attorney-selection blog authority checks

Effective September 14, 2026. Applies across AMPLIFY clients to Best of, best lawyer, why hire, who should I hire, choosing counsel and similar selection articles.

The article must contain a substantial client-named H2 section before its FAQs. Research experience, awards, professional associations and leadership, results, client reviews, authored publications and media coverage, teaching/speaking, and certifications. Use original sources, correct person/year attribution, direct citations and practical relevance. A generic contact section is insufficient. Disclose evidence that remains unavailable after research. Keep peer ratings separate from client reviews, settlements separate from verdicts, authored work separate from expert mentions, and awards separate from specialty certifications. Do not choose between conflicting result figures without verification.

`blog-prompt.ts` requires this research and preserves it during shortening. `generation-faq-repair.ts` also preserves the section during FAQ corrections. `attorney-authority.ts` identifies selection topics and validates the section structure. `attorney-authority-review.ts` independently verifies evidence with web research and accounts for all eight categories. Only a passing review is cached, keyed to the section, client, topic and standard version for 24 hours. Changed content requires another review.

The check runs before Google Doc creation, on the formatted WordPress content during preparation, on the saved WordPress draft before marking preparation complete, and before publication or scheduling. Checking the saved title and content makes the cached evidence review reusable at publication despite Google Doc conversion. Generation can research and repair a failed section twice; unresolved failures stop progression. Preparation failures remain visible and require correction. Intake-only draft saves are not preparation approval. These checks never authorize publication.

Source audits run as durable background responses. Generation polls the same audit, and the WordPress worker resumes pending verification without consuming upload-failure attempts. A slow research job must not restart simply because a server request ends. The final-review page runs a read-only saved-draft preflight and polls pending audits while visible. Its approval button stays disabled until verification passes. The preflight returns before all publication, scheduling and publication-tracking mutations; publication still requires final approval and checks the saved content again.

Regression coverage in `scripts/attorney-authority.test.mjs` runs during every production build and covers the prior generic Billy section, missing categories, unsupported claims, cache invalidation, title variants, and all three route integration points.
