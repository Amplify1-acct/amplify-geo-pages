# AMPLIFY Hiring and Best Blog Standard

Approved by Matthew September 15, 2026. Applies to hiring, “Best,” lawyer-selection and counsel-comparison blogs. Ordinary informational blogs keep their own format.

## Order once

Capture client, practice area, topic/location, featured lawyer, editorial slot and notes. The optional **Lawyer to feature** field persists through tracker, retries, revisions, Aron review, WordPress preparation and final verification. For RDCY nursing-home articles, Sean C. Do not demand biographies of every colleague. Mention co-counsel only where a cited matter requires correct attribution. Preserve the selected lawyer on revisions.

## Article pattern

1. Answer the hiring question directly, without an unsupported universal “best” claim.
2. Explain topic-specific experience, evidence, experts, trial readiness, communication and fees.
3. Give useful consultation questions and immediate practical steps.
4. Include one substantial client-named authority H2 before the FAQs.
5. Develop the firm section as a coherent hiring narrative: experience and credentials; what recognition covers; handling difficult cases; professional responsibilities; service/mentoring; published work and teaching; review context; consultation fit.
6. Explain why the evidence matters to the prospective client. Use readable paragraphs and group related honors. Do not write a source-audit report or append the same limitation after every sentence.
7. Preserve ten jurisdiction-specific sourced FAQs and matching schema at preparation.

The firm section must cover eight categories: experience, awards, associations, results, reviews, publications, teaching and certifications. Relevant evidence may be grouped into the narrative. A factual and clearly scoped selection is acceptable; do not require an exhaustive lifetime bibliography or testimonial inventory. A generic CTA or two paragraphs do not satisfy the standard. A minimum length is a guard against an empty section, not proof of quality or a reason to pad copy.

## Research once, verify updates

Use the saved client/lawyer source pack first, then inspect original sources for changes and topic fit. Sean's initial source pack is in `src/lib/hiring-blog-reference.ts`. It includes the approved AAJ/Trial bibliography and Scholar discovery route as well as primary professional sources.

For each used claim, identify the lawyer/firm, source, date, category and relevance. Verify original publisher authorship. Google Scholar records are bibliographic records; do not describe professional columns as peer-reviewed medical research. Include relevant publications even when a search of the firm homepage misses them.

Keep client reviews, peer ratings, firm-selected testimonials and historical predecessor-firm listings distinct. Numeric ratings require counts and verification dates. Do not add numbers merely to satisfy a checker. Certifications require the actual certifying body and accurate field. Describe a cited result accurately; a reported settlement, jury verdict, final judgment and collected recovery are different facts. State a material limitation once near the relevant evidence. Do not make unsupported local-office claims.

## One bounded review, targeted correction

The reviewer checks the selected lawyer/topic scope and all eight categories. It verifies every stated claim and reports all material issues together. Missing categories, unsupported claims, wrong attribution, misleading results and missing material source support fail. Optional additional awards, old reviews or duplicative talks do not turn a supported category into a failure.

A reviewer must not progressively require an encyclopedic biography. A nonnumeric accurate outcome need not acquire a dollar amount. An accessible publisher byline controls attribution; a failed fetch alone is not proof that authorship is false. Flag a material contradiction and resolve it; do not guess.

Corrections edit the identified section and retain unrelated approved copy. Saved editor corrections are pinned to the provider response/client and are never replaced by automatic full rewrites. All corrected content still passes normal checks. Original provider text and editor backups remain available.

## Completion and approvals

Image generation and review use the same practice-specific scene. A hiring or “Best” title does not override nursing-home, medical, vehicle or other subject relevance with a generic consultation room. Retry prompts carry the previous rejection reasons, while retaining the required subject, location plausibility, no-text/no-logo rules and three-attempt limit. An explicit retry after correcting the cause resumes the existing approved document and draft.

Keep generation, source review, Aron writing approval, user preparation authorization, verified WordPress draft, final publication authorization, scheduling/publication, links and GSC statuses separate. No source audit grants human approval. Do not say a retry or queued job is complete. Show the actual error when a material issue remains.

## Regression protection

- The full cited firm section must win over an earlier heading containing one attorney surname.
- Generic sections and missing evidence categories must still fail.
- Selected-lawyer scope persists through all workflow stages.
- Scholar-indexed writing is included as professional publication evidence with original-publisher citations.
- Editorial selection and source accuracy are separate: optional additions must not fail supported content.

Maintain this document, the source pack, writer/reviewer instructions, and tests together when changing this workflow.

## Tracker freshness

Open trackers refresh remote state every 30 seconds and on tab focus. A newer remote correction replaces an old browser error; newer local edits survive. Deletion markers are honored. The user should not have to order the blog again to see the status of an existing corrected draft. A newly deployed browser fix requires one page reload to load the new app code.
